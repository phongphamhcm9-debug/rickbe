Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-02

This application ("automatic upload contents") is a private, personal-use tool operated solely by its owner. It is not distributed to the public and has no other users besides the owner.

The application connects to the owner's own social media accounts (including TikTok) via each platform's official OAuth login, solely to upload video files that the owner has personally created. The application does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data belonging to any third party.

Access tokens obtained through OAuth are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are used only to authorize video uploads to the owner's own connected accounts. No data is transmitted to any party other than the relevant platform's official API (e.g. TikTok, YouTube, Facebook).

The owner may disconnect any connected account at any time, which deletes the locally stored token.
